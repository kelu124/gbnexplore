## LIFE-myBUILDINGisGREEN: Green Roof Retrofit at Falcão Primary School

### **I. INITIATIVE OVERVIEW AND IDENTIFICATION**

#### **Basic Information**
- **Initiative Title:** LIFE-myBUILDINGisGREEN – Green Roof Retrofit at Falcão Primary School
- **Alternative Names:** MyBuildingIsGreen, Falcão School Greening Project, Nature-Based Solutions at Falcão School
- **Location:** Porto, Mello Falcão neighborhood, Porto District, Portugal
- **Geographic Coordinates:** 41.1579° N, 8.6291° W (approximate, based on Porto city center)
- **Timeline:** 
  - Project Start: 2017 (LIFE Programme launch)
  - Retrofit Implementation: 2021–2022 (on-site interventions at Falcão School)
  - Ongoing Monitoring: 2022–present
  - Completion Status: Completed (with ongoing evaluation and dissemination)
- **Project Scale:** 
  - Area: 700 m² of green roofs and façades
  - Population Affected: ~300 students and staff at Falcão Primary School
  - Investment Value: Part of a €4.5 million LIFE Programme budget (multi-city, multi-site)
- **Leading Organization(s):** 
  - Câmara Municipal do Porto (Porto City Council)
  - GO Porto (Porto’s municipal environmental agency)
  - Porto Ambiente (municipal environmental services)
  - Consortium partners: Universities, research institutes, and private sector actors across Spain and Portugal
- **Funding Sources:** 
  - European Union LIFE Programme (primary funder)
  - Municipal budgets (Porto City Council)
  - Research grants and in-kind contributions from academic partners

#### **Executive Summary**
The LIFE-myBUILDINGisGREEN project at Falcão Primary School in Porto, Portugal, is a flagship urban adaptation initiative that demonstrates the integration of nature-based solutions (NBS) into existing educational infrastructure. The project retrofitted the school with extensive green roofs, photovoltaic panels, and green façades to improve bioclimatic comfort, reduce energy demand, and enhance local biodiversity. By combining advanced energy modeling, real-time monitoring, and participatory design, the initiative serves as a model for climate-resilient urban schools in Southern Europe. The project’s success lies in its multi-benefit approach: it not only improves indoor environmental quality for students and staff but also contributes to urban heat island mitigation, rainwater management, and community engagement. The Falcão School has become a living laboratory for sustainable urban development, influencing policy and practice in Porto and beyond.
