## Green-Campus Living Lab (TU Dublin)

### **I. INITIATIVE OVERVIEW AND IDENTIFICATION**

#### **Basic Information**
- **Initiative Title:** Green-Campus Living Lab
- **Alternative Names:** TU Dublin Living Lab, Green-Campus Programme, Green Flag Initiative
- **Location:** Dublin, Ireland (multi-campus university: Grangegorman, Aungier Street, Bolton Street, Blanchardstown, Tallaght)
- **Geographic Coordinates:** Varies by campus (e.g., Grangegorman: 53.3525° N, 6.2750° W)
- **Timeline:** 
  - 2007: TU Dublin (then DIT) joined the An Taisce Green-Campus Programme
  - 2023: Awarded Green Flag for all campuses under a single application
  - Ongoing: Living Lab activities, annual Green Week, Open Call for projects, and continuous alignment with Climate Action Roadmap
- **Project Scale:** 
  - 5 campuses, 185 acres of grounds, 204,300 m² of accommodation, 47 buildings
  - Over 50 external partners, 1,621 participants in Green Week 2025
- **Leading Organization(s):** TU Dublin (Green-Campus Committee, Societal Engagement Team, Decarbonisation and Sustainability Education units)
- **Funding Sources:** Internal university funding, micro-grants (up to €20,000 for Open Call projects), support from An Taisce, collaboration with EPA and IUA

#### **Executive Summary**
The Green-Campus Living Lab at TU Dublin is a campus-wide, multi-stakeholder initiative that transforms the university into a real-world laboratory for sustainability innovation. By integrating academic research, operational practice, and community engagement, the Living Lab fosters environmental management, education, and action across all campuses. The initiative is structured around the An Taisce Green-Campus Programme and TU Dublin’s Climate Action Roadmap, aiming to reduce the university’s environmental footprint, enhance energy efficiency, and promote a culture of sustainability. Key activities include real-world experimentation, participatory digital platforms, and iterative prototyping, with outcomes such as the Green Flag award, increased replicability of sustainable interventions, and data-driven decision-making. The Living Lab not only addresses immediate campus sustainability challenges but also serves as a model for other institutions seeking to align with climate action mandates and foster a sustainable future.
