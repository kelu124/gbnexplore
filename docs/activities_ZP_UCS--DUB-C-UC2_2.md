# Executive summary## Ethos Engineering Living Lab

### **I. INITIATIVE OVERVIEW AND IDENTIFICATION**

#### **Basic Information**
- **Initiative Title:** Ethos Engineering Living Lab
- **Alternative Names:** Living Lab, iLab (Intelligent Lab), Ethos Digital Living Lab
- **Location:** Sandyford, Dublin, Ireland
- **Geographic Coordinates:** 53.2707° N, 6.2181° W (approximate, based on Sandyford, Dublin)
- **Timeline:** 
  - November 2021: Launch of Living Lab at Ethos HQ
  - August 2022: First business globally to achieve WELL Performance Rating for Living Lab
  - Ongoing: Continuous development and client service adaptation
- **Project Scale:** 
  - Headquarters building (approx. 2,000–3,000 sqm, based on typical office HQs)
  - 250+ staff directly impacted; broader impact via client services and industry influence
- **Leading Organization(s):** Ethos Engineering (Ireland’s largest MEP consultancy), in partnership with Spaceti (IoT sensor provider), Autodesk (digital twin platform)
- **Funding Sources:** Internal investment by Ethos Engineering, with technology partnerships and client-driven service development

#### **Executive Summary**
The Ethos Engineering Living Lab is a pioneering smart building initiative that transforms the company’s Dublin headquarters into a real-world laboratory for sustainable, data-driven urban environments. By deploying a comprehensive network of IoT sensors—measuring air quality, occupancy, energy use, and acoustics—the project enables real-time monitoring and management of building performance. The Living Lab integrates these data streams into a unified digital platform, leveraging digital twin technology and mobile engagement tools to optimize space usage, enhance occupant well-being, and drive progress toward net zero targets. The initiative has achieved global recognition, notably as the first business worldwide to earn the WELL Performance Rating, validating its commitment to health, sustainability, and innovation. Beyond its internal impact, the Living Lab serves as a scalable model for clients and the broader industry, demonstrating how digital technologies can create healthier, more efficient, and resilient urban spaces. Its approach is particularly relevant in the context of hybrid work, flexible building use, and the urgent need for climate action in the built environment.

### **II. CONTEXTUAL ANALYSIS**

#### **Urban Context**
- **City Profile:** Dublin is Ireland’s capital and largest city, with a population of approximately 1.4 million in the greater metropolitan area. It is a major economic, cultural, and technological hub in Europe, known for its strong presence in the tech, finance, and professional services sectors. Dublin’s economy is highly service-oriented, with a growing emphasis on innovation and sustainability. The city faces challenges related to urban density, traffic congestion, and the need for climate-resilient infrastructure.
- **Neighborhood/District Characteristics:** Sandyford is a key business and technology district in south Dublin, home to numerous multinational corporations, tech firms, and professional services. The area is characterized by modern office developments, good transport links, and a mix of residential and commercial uses. The Ethos HQ is situated within this dynamic environment, reflecting the district’s focus on innovation and sustainability.
- **Historical Development Patterns:** Sandyford has evolved from a suburban area to a major business park, with significant investment in office and mixed-use developments since the 1980s. The district is known for its high-quality office stock and proximity to Dublin’s tech corridor.
- **Current Land Use Composition:** Predominantly commercial and office, with some residential and retail components. The area is well-served by public transport and amenities.
- **Demographic Profile:** The workforce in Sandyford is highly skilled, with a mix of ages and backgrounds, reflecting Dublin’s diverse and international population. Income levels are generally above the national average.
- **Existing Infrastructure Quality and Accessibility:** Sandyford benefits from modern infrastructure, including high-speed internet, public transport (Luas tram, bus services), and cycling facilities. The area is well-connected to Dublin city center and airport.
- **Environmental Conditions and Constraints:** The district faces typical urban environmental challenges, including air quality concerns, noise pollution, and the need for sustainable energy and water management. Climate change adaptation is a growing priority for local authorities and businesses.

#### **Pre-Initiative Conditions**
- **Problems/Challenges Identified:** 
  - Inefficient building operations, with limited real-time data on energy use, occupancy, and environmental quality.
  - Difficulty in optimizing space usage and resource allocation, especially with the rise of hybrid work.
  - Lack of actionable insights for improving occupant health and well-being.
  - Challenges in meeting sustainability and net zero targets, with reliance on manual data collection and reporting.
- **Triggering Events:** 
  - The global push for net zero emissions and sustainable urban development.
  - The impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on workplace design and occupant health.
  - The launch of the WELL Performance Rating, which provided a new benchmark for building performance and occupant experience.
- **Stakeholder Landscape:** 
  - Ethos Engineering (lead organization, with internal teams for digital services, sustainability, and operations).
  - Employees and building occupants (direct beneficiaries).
  - Technology partners (Spaceti, Autodesk).
  - Clients and industry peers (indirect beneficiaries and adopters).
  - Local authorities and sustainability organizations (regulatory and advocacy stakeholders).

#### **Policy and Regulatory Environment**
- **Governance Framework:** The initiative is led by Ethos Engineering, with support from technology partners and industry organizations. Local authorities and sustainability bodies provide regulatory oversight and certification.
- **Relevant Policies:** 
  - Irish government commitments to net zero by 2050 and carbon reduction targets for 2030.
  - EU directives on energy efficiency and building performance.
  - WELL Building Standard and WELL Performance Rating as industry benchmarks.

### **IV. DESIGN AND IMPLEMENTATION APPROACH**

#### **Project Design**
- **Conceptual Framework:** The Living Lab is based on principles of smart building design, data-driven decision-making, and occupant-centric innovation. It integrates environmental, social, and technological considerations to create a holistic approach to building management.
- **Physical/Spatial Design:** The initiative involves retrofitting the existing headquarters with IoT sensors and digital platforms, without major physical changes to the building. The focus is on optimizing the use of existing space and resources.
- **Scope and Phasing:** 
  - Phase 1: Deployment of IoT sensors for air quality, occupancy, energy, and acoustics.
  - Phase 2: Integration of data into a unified dashboard and digital twin platform.
  - Phase 3: Development of mobile engagement app and client service offerings.

#### **Technologies and Innovations**
- **Technologies Deployed:** 
  - IoT sensor network (environmental, desk occupancy, people-counting, energy, acoustics).
  - Digital twin platform (Autodesk Tandem).
  - Mobile engagement app (tenant interaction, room/desk bookings, touchless access).
- **Innovation Type:** Technological (IoT, digital twin), social (occupant engagement), and process-oriented (data-driven management).
- **Integration Approach:** The technologies are integrated through a unified IoT platform, with data visualized in a digital twin and accessible via mobile app. This enables seamless interaction between building systems, occupants, and management.

#### **Implementation Methodology**
- **Procurement and Contracting:** Ethos Engineering led the procurement, partnering with Spaceti for sensors and Autodesk for digital twin technology.
- **Stakeholder Engagement:** Employees and building occupants were involved in the design and testing of the system, with feedback incorporated into the platform.
- **Participatory Elements:** The mobile app allows occupants to interact with the building and provide feedback, fostering a sense of ownership and engagement.
- **Project Management:** Ethos used internal project management structures, with cross-functional teams for digital services, sustainability, and operations.

#### **Financing Model**
- **Budget and Costs:** Internal investment by Ethos Engineering, with technology partnerships reducing upfront costs.
- **Funding Mix:** Primarily private investment, with potential for client-driven revenue through service offerings.
- **Revenue Mechanisms:** Service fees for client implementations, potential for data-driven consulting.
- **Financial Sustainability:** The initiative is designed to be self-sustaining through internal efficiencies and client services, with ongoing investment in technology and innovation.
