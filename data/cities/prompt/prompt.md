**Writing Guidelines:**
- Use concrete examples and specific details rather than generalizations
- Include actual statistics and data where relevant
- Balance quantitative information with qualitative observations
- Write in present tense for current conditions, past tense for trends
- Use active voice and vary sentence structure
- Include 2-3 brief quotes or paraphrased community voices where appropriate
- Aim for approximately 600-700 words per page
- Bold key terms and important findings for easy scanning
- Ensure each section flows logically to the next

Please write this report with authentic detail that brings the neighborhood to life while maintaining analytical rigor. The tone should be informative yet engaging, critical yet constructive, and always respectful of the community being described.
